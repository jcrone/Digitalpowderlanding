MySkiSwap

The marketplace for pre-loved ski & snowboard gear.

ConsignGear

Consignment, without the clipboard.

ConsignGear is consignment management for gear shops and ski swap organizers. Check in items, track inventory, sell, and pay out consignors — all from one place instead of spreadsheets and paper tags.

  • Quick item intake with printable tags
  • Automatic consignor splits, payouts, and statements
  • Real-time inventory for shops and swap events

About us

A small team of skiers who write software.

Digital Powder started with a simple frustration: buying and selling used ski gear was harder than it needed to be. Classifieds were cluttered, swap meets ran on paper, and great equipment sat in garages.

So we built MySkiSwap. Talking with shops and swap organizers along the way, we kept hearing the same thing — consignment is still run on clipboards and spreadsheets. ConsignGear is our answer.
